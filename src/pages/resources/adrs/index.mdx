Drafts live in the repo under `drafts/adrs/` until they are accepted.

## Status definitions

- **DRAFT** — Under authorship, not published here
- **Proposed** — Open for review
- **Accepted** — Approved and binding
- **Rejected** / **Deprecated** / **Superseded** — Historical

## Proposing a new ADR

1. Open a PR using the template in [ADR 1](/resources/adrs/adr/1)
2. Leave at least one week for review
3. Security reviewers sign off on crypto-related ADRs
4. Only maintainers change status to Accepted or Rejected
