---
title: "DAOs"
description: "Terp Network DAOs and subDAOs"
---

Terp governance runs through on-chain DAOs: a main DAO holding the community mandate plus focused subDAOs (workstreams) that propose and execute within a budget.

- **Informal workstream** — a group organizing around an idea, unfunded.
- **Proposed** — the workstream has submitted a budget proposal on-chain.
- **Active** — funded and executing; progress is accountable to voters.
- **Inactive** — discontinued, no longer funded.

Starting one means gathering momentum publicly ([Discord](https://discord.terp.network)), then putting a budget to a vote like any other [proposal](./proposals). There is no off-chain approval step that substitutes for the on-chain vote.
