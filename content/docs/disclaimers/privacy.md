This policy explains what information is involved when you use terp.network, and any subdomain involved or associated (together, “the Service”), and how we are expecting to handle it.

### What we actually deal with

Almost everything that happens on Terp Network is public by design. Transactions, messages, and other activity you broadcast to the network are visible to anyone. We do not collect private personal profiles, accounts, or private messages beyond what is already public on-chain or voluntarily sent to our public servers.

The only additional data that appears is standard technical information that any website or server naturally sees:

- IP address  
- Browser type and version  
- Device type and operating system  
- Time and date of the request  

This information is generated automatically when your device talks to our servers. We do not combine it with other data to identify you, and we do not keep it longer than needed for basic operations and security (normally up to 30 days in server logs).

### How the servers are run

We host the Service on open, permissionless compute markets (such as Akash) or on infrastructure we operate ourselves. The operators of those compute resources do not have direct access to the data stored on the instances in the normal course of operation.

Because some of the underlying infrastructure may be located in or controlled by entities subject to U.S. law, there is a theoretical possibility that U.S. authorities could attempt to compel access under laws such as FISA Section 702 or the CLOUD Act. If that residual risk is unacceptable to you, the only fully safe choice is not to use the Service.

### Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold that can be linked to you. In practice this is limited, because the technical logs are not tied to personal identities under normal operations.

If any personal data is processed on the basis of consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. You can also ask for correction or deletion where the data is inaccurate or no longer needed.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at **team.terp@protonmail.com**. We may need to verify that you are the person making the request.

### When we might share data

We only share information if required by law or a valid legal request, or if it is necessary to protect the security of the Service or the safety of people using it.

### Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the Service and its logs secure. No system connected to the internet is perfectly safe, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Other sites

Links on the Service may take you to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are their own; we recommend reading their policies.

### Changes

If we update this policy we will post the new version here and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

### Contact

Questions about this policy: **team.terp@protonmail.com**
